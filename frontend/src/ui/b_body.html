Strategic preference planner

Build a budget-feasible plan across every selected course without guessing what other students will place. The University publishes the preference-matching mechanism and live counts of interested students, but not historical clearing prices or other students' own preference points. This planner therefore shows an opening bid and a personal ceiling—never a made-up win probability.
