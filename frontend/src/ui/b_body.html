This simulator assumes serious competition

No historical SNU clearing-price or bidder-count data exists — this is the first cohort. Every course is assumed oversubscribed until the platform proves otherwise. Recommendations are conservative model outputs, not guarantees. Enter live bidder counts as soon as the platform shows them — a real number replaces the assumption entirely.
